QUICK GUIDE

How to use JewelFX

  1. Click Start camera and allow browser camera permission.
  2. Face the camera in even, bright lighting.
  3. Switch jewellery pieces on or off and choose a metal finish.
  4. Use the size slider to adjust the overlays.
  5. Capture a photo or record a short video.

Face tracking downloads a small MediaPipe model from Google's hosting on first use, so internet access is needed.
